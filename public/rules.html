Rules, Live Eval & Controls

Everything you need to know to play, read the live evaluation bar, and review games.

How the Live Evaluation Bar Works

When enabled in the lobby, a vertical bar appears beside the chessboard. White's portion fills from the bottom (or top if the board is flipped). Positive scores like +1.8 favor White; negative scores like -2.3 favor Black; M2 indicates a forced checkmate in 2 moves. You can toggle the bar anytime with the Hide/Show Eval button.

Full Chess Rules Supported

  • Castling: Kingside (O-O) and Queenside (O-O-O) when neither king nor rook has moved and squares are unattacked.
  • En Passant: Immediate diagonal pawn capture after an opponent's two-square pawn advance.
  • Pawn Promotion: Choose Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight when a pawn reaches the 8th (or 1st) rank.
  • Draws: Automatic detection of Stalemate, Threefold Repetition, 50-Move Rule, and Insufficient Material, plus mutual Draw Offers.

Playing Across Different Devices

Click Play Online, create a room, and copy the invite link from the arena sidebar. Send that link to your friend on their phone or laptop—as soon as both players open the link, the clocks start automatically.
